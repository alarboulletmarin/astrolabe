# Today

The home page, and the one Astrolabe opens on. It tells where things stand, in figures.

<!-- screenshot: the Today page on made-up data -->

## What it shows

- **This week** against your **median week**: how much time (or distance, for one sport) you have done since Monday, and what a usual week holds.
- **Streak**: the run of weeks with at least one outing, and the longest one so far.
- **The year so far** against the same date a year before.
- **Six months week by week**: the time of each week across sports, stacked by sport. With one sport chosen, the bars are its distance (its time for a sport without distance, such as strength training).
- **A year day by day**: how busy each day was.
- **Latest outings**: the last few, one line each. A click opens the [activity](activity.md).
- **Records of the last two weeks**, or "No record broken in the last two weeks." when none was broken. See [Records](records.md).

Sports mix, so across all of them the common measure is time: two kilometres of swimming are not two of cycling. With one sport chosen in the chips above the charts, the chart is told in kilometres, when that sport has a distance.

## Good to know

- With no activity yet, the page says so and points to the synchronisation, at the bottom of the rail.
- Nothing here gives advice. Astrolabe states where you stand, nothing more.
