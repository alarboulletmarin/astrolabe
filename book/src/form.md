# Form

Load, fatigue and freshness, worked out from the heart rate of your outings. A statement of figures: no advice, no verdict.

<!-- screenshot: Form, six months -->

## What it shows

- **Fatigue**: the load averaged over about a week.
- **Form**: the load averaged over about six weeks.
- **Freshness**: form minus fatigue, at the end of the day. **Freshness today** gives today's, and how the load of the week stands against the week before ("no load last week" when there was none).
- Two charts, **Form and fatigue** and **Form minus fatigue**, over **3 months**, **6 months**, **1 year** or **All**.

The section **Where these figures come from** says how each figure is computed, in the app.

## Good to know

- The load of an outing comes from its heart rate, against your maximum rate (the second highest of your outings' maxima, each the highest rate held over five samples, so that one sensor spike does not set it). Until one is known the page says "Maximum heart rate: not known yet."
- Outings without heart rate count for nothing. With none at all: "No outing with a heart rate yet: no load can be worked out."
- A series shorter than a week draws no curve.
