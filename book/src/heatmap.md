# Heatmap

All your tracks on one map, darker where you go most.

<!-- screenshot: the heatmap of a town -->

## Using it

- **Sports** and **Year** filter what is drawn. **All** and **Any time** show everything.
- A count tells how many outings with a track are drawn, and their distance.
- A click (not a drag) on the map lists the activities that pass there, with the number of passes. A click opens one.
- Drag to move the map; the wheel zooms around the pointer.

## Good to know

- Without any track: "No GPS track here yet: they arrive with the synchronisation." While files download, the page counts what is "still to download."
- Indoor activities have no track and are left out.
