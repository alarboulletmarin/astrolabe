# Records

Your best efforts, by kind of sport.

<!-- screenshot: Records, road running -->

## Boards

Boards of best times, by kind: **Road running** (1 km, 5 km, 10 km, half marathon, marathon), **Trail** apart, **Cycling** and **Swimming**, plus the longest outings and the biggest climbs. Each row shows a podium of three; a click on a row unfolds the ten best, each opening its activity.

The times are *best times anywhere inside an outing, like a stopwatch started on the way*: a fast 5 km inside a half marathon counts.

## Filters

**All sports** or one sport, and **All years** or one year.

## Good to know

- Times over a distance are worked out from the tracks. While files are still downloading, the page says so and fills in as they arrive.
- A distance never covered reads "not yet".
- A record set in the last two weeks also shows on [Today](dashboard.md).
