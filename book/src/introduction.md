# Introduction

Astrolabe is a desktop journal of your sport activities, read from your Garmin account. It
downloads the list of your activities and the file of each one (GPS track, heart rate, laps), keeps
them on your machine, and opens instantly, online or not.

It shows where you stand: this week against your usual week, your records, your form, every track
on one map. It states figures; it never tells you what to do.

- [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md): every error message and what to do about it.
