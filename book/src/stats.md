# Statistics

Any measure, grouping, period and set of sports.

<!-- screenshot: Statistics, bars by month -->

## The sentence

The query is a sentence. Each bold word opens a menu:

> **Time** **by week** **last 12 months** **all sports** **no comparison**

| Word | Choices |
|---|---|
| Measure | **Time**, **Distance**, **Elevation**, **Outings**, **Pace**, **Average HR** |
| Grouping | **by day**, **by week**, **by month**, **by year** |
| Period | **This year**, **Last 4 weeks**, **Last 3 months**, **Last 6 months**, **Last 12 months**, **Since the start**, or **Pick dates…** |
| Sports | **All sports**, or any of the sports you did (several at once) |
| Comparison | **No comparison**, **With the period before**, **With the same period a year before** |

A long period grouped by day is a wall of slivers: Astrolabe then picks a larger grouping by itself (week beyond 120 days, month beyond 800).

## Two charts

**Bars** show one bar per group; **Running total** shows the running total day by day ("Distance, accumulated day by day"…). A click on the chart selects a group, and its activities are listed below.

Above the chart, a line gives the totals of the period.

## Good to know

- Pace across sports would mean nothing: with several sports, it is told sport by sport.
- With no outing in the period, the page says "No outings in this period."
