# An activity

The page of one outing. A click in the [journal](journal.md), the [calendar](calendar.md), the [heatmap](heatmap.md) or a list opens it.

<!-- screenshot: an activity, map and curves -->

## What it shows

- **The map** of the track, on OpenStreetMap tiles. Without GPS, the page says "No GPS position: an indoor activity, or one without GPS."
- **The curves**: **Pace** (or **Speed**, by sport), **Heart rate** and **Altitude**. They share one pointer: pointing at one points at the others and on the map.
- **Per kilometre** (**Per 100 m** for swimming): the splits, one bar each.
- **Laps**: the laps the device recorded.
- **Before this one**: the previous outings of the same sport, one bar each; a click opens one. The page says where this one stands: "Your longest, out of…", "Your shortest, out of…" or "Your first in this sport."

The file of an outing is read when it arrives; until then the page says "Reading the track…". If reading fails, **Retry** tries again.

## Moving around

| Key | Does |
|---|---|
| `←`, `→` | the older or newer outing (the buttons **‹ Older** and **Newer ›** do the same) |
| `Esc` | back to the page before |

Going from one outing to the next replaces the entry in the history: **Back** returns to where you came from, not through each step. See [Navigation](navigation.md).

An activity deleted on Garmin and gone from the journal reads "This activity no longer exists."
