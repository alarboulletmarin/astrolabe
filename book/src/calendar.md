# Calendar

Your outings in the cells of the days.

<!-- screenshot: the Calendar, month view -->

## Three views

The buttons **Week**, **Month** and **Year** choose what is shown.

- **Week** and **month**: each day lists what was done, an outing at a time, as a chip with a sport icon and a figure (the distance, or the time where there is none). A cell too full ends with "+ n"; a narrow cell keeps the icons only. Where there is room, the weekly totals sit beside the month.
- **Year**: every day as a small cell, darker the busier it was. A year with nothing reads "Nothing that year."

The chips **All** and one per sport filter the outings shown.

## Moving around

| Key | Does |
|---|---|
| `←`, `→` | the previous or next week, month or year |
| `T` | today (the **Today** button does the same) |

A click on an outing opens its [activity](activity.md).
