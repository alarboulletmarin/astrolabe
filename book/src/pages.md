# The pages

The rail, on the left, opens each page. The arrows at its top go back and forward, as in a browser.

## Today

The home page. This week against your median week, the run of weeks with at least one outing, the
year so far against the same date a year before. Then six months week by week (time across sports,
stacked by sport; distance for one sport), up to a year day by day, the latest outings and the
records of the last two weeks.

Across sports the common measure is time: two kilometres of swimming are not two of cycling.

## Journal

Every activity, week by week, with the week's totals (kilometres sport by sport). Search by name or
sport, filter by sport, and choose the columns: distance, time, pace, the gap to your usual pace on
like outings, elevation, climb per kilometre, heart rate, calories, rank, the time in each heart
rate zone and the altitude profile. Columns that do not fit fold away, the least useful first.

A click, or `Enter` on the selected row, opens the activity.

## Activity

The map of the track; the pace, heart rate and elevation curves (pointing at one points at the
others and on the map); the splits per kilometre and the laps; where the outing stands among the
previous ones. `←` and `→` go to the older and newer activity, `Esc` goes back.

## Calendar

Your outings in the days, by week, month or year. `←` and `→` move by one period, `T` comes back to
today.

## Statistics

Pick the measure, the grouping (day, week, month, year), the period and the sports. Bars or a
running total, compared with the period before or the year before. A click on a group lists its
activities.

## Form

Load, fatigue and freshness worked out from the heart rate of your outings, with how each figure is
computed. A statement of figures: no advice, no verdict.

## Records

Best efforts on the road (1 km, 5 km, 10 km, half marathon, marathon), trail apart, cycling and
swimming, the longest outings and the biggest climbs, for one year or all of them.

## Heatmap

All your tracks on one map, darker where you go most. Filter by sport and year; a click on the map
lists the activities that pass there.

## Settings

The Garmin key, the progress of the synchronisation, the map tiles, the theme and language, and
where your data are kept.
